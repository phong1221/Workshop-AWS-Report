# Event 2: Hackathon "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week"

- **Event Name:** Agentic AI Build Week powered by GenAI Fund
- **Date & Time:** 09:00, July 25, 2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee

---

### 4.2.1. Event Objectives
- **Agentic AI Application:** Challenge building practical self-operating AI products.
- **AWS Ecosystem Optimization:** Leverage AWS cloud services for rapid project development and deployment (within 24-48 hours).
- **Solving Real-World Problems:** Focus on resolving practical enterprise and societal pain points rather than broad theory.
- **Comprehensive Development:** Practice environment to refine teamwork, presentation, time-pressure management, and network deeply with the First Cloud Journey community.

### 4.2.2. Key Highlights

#### Practical Experience Sharing
Teams emphasized that success in a hackathon depends not just on complex technology, but on accurately identifying business pain points and implementing practical prototype solutions to convince judges.

#### Presentations by 4 Teams:
- **One Team:** Focused on aggregating and analyzing fragmented data from financial reports and corporate documents. They built an AI Agent with a simple chatbot UI, giving strategy and risk teams a holistic corporate overview to support business decisions.
- **Team BL:** Introduced SA Professional AI Native App for Solution Architects. The app automatically analyzes business requirements, draws architecture diagrams, estimates costs, and generates Infrastructure as Code (IaC). This resolves extreme time pressure when clients request system designs within days.
- **Team 2K:** Presented "Sheper", applying YOLO and ByteTrack technology to analyze crowd movement via cameras at airports or events. The AI calculates real-time flow across areas, proposing crowd and traffic congestion management solutions.
- **Team Six Piller:** Presented Adaptive Workflow Engine in finance, targeting Anti-Money Laundering (AML). The solution automatically collects and verifies data across multiple systems, summarizing suspicious fraud alerts. It incorporates a human-in-the-loop model where AI processes massive input data and humans make final assessments.

### 4.2.3. Key Takeaways
- **Problem-Solving Mindset:** The biggest lesson is not using complex tech, but accurately identifying business pain points. Teams proved a good product starts from real needs (e.g., Team BL solving architecture drafting pressure, or Team Six Piller optimizing AML workflows).
- **Infinite Potential of Agentic AI & Automation:** Team presentations demonstrated the power of AI shifting from "assistance" to "autonomous operation". Automatically writing IaC code, summarizing financial reports, or analyzing crowd flow in real-time opens new operational optimization perspectives.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Model:** Team Six Piller offered a practical lesson: in sensitive domains like finance, AI doesn't need to replace humans entirely. Combining AI (processing huge data volumes) with humans (final decision-making) is the safest and most effective approach.

### 4.2.4. Personal Experience & Impressions
- **Fast-Paced & Creative:** The event carried the true spirit of a Hackathon under high time pressure (24-48 hours), acting as a powerful catalyst for breakthrough ideas to materialize rapidly.
- **Deep Community Value:** Beyond a competition, "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week" was a grand networking day. Witnessing live discussions and feedback among developers, Solution Architects, and judges from First Cloud Journey provided immense inspiration and motivation.
